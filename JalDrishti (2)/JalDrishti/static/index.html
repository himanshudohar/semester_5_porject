From a place name to a clear answer in three steps

Pick your spot

Search a village or tap the exact field on the map.

We read the land

The model looks at rock, soil, slope, rainfall, streams and how nearby wells performed.

You get a likelihood

Low, Medium or High, with the main reasons. It is an estimate, not a promise.

What this tool cannot tell you

  • It cannot promise water. A High result means conditions look favourable, not that every borewell will succeed.
  • It does not know depth or yield. How deep to drill and how much water you will get needs an on-site survey.
  • Get a survey first. Ask a hydrogeologist and book a resistivity survey at the exact spot before you drill.
